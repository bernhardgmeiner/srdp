Exam timer

Rehearse the real thing: the exam clock for your school type, with a sensible split across the tasks. Start it when you sit down to a full practice paper – beating the clock is a skill you train, not a talent.

2 tasks · 120 minutes · no dictionary
120:00

A sensible split

You pick the order on the day, but a plan stops one task eating the others. Adjust it to your strengths, then defend it against the clock.

  1. 65 minTask 1 – the long text (about 400 words)
  2. 40 minTask 2 – the short text (about 250 words)
  3. 15 minRead through and correct both
This is a rehearsal tool – the real exam has no pause button. At AHS no dictionaries are allowed anywhere in the exam. Do a couple of full runs before the Klausur and the real clock stops being scary.
