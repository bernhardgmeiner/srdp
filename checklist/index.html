My word count is within ±10% of the number in the task (for a 250-word task, 225–275).
I started every body paragraph with a clear topic sentence.
I used at least five different linking words (Furthermore, However, Consequently …).
I did not copy sentences from the task or the material. I put figures and ideas in my own words.High priority

About this site

A free, independent site for the text types of the written English Matura (AHS and BHS, B2), from the 5th class to the Matura. Made by an English teacher. It is not an official document of the BMB or of any education authority.
