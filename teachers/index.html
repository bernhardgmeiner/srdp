About

For teachers

What this site is, and how to use it in your classroom. Short version: take it, it’s free.

What this is

A free, independent revision site for the writing part of the English Matura (AHS and BHS, B2), built by Bernhard Gmeiner, an English teacher in Vienna, with the help of Claude Cowork. It follows the official SRDP documents – the B2 assessment grid (2023 revision), the text type characteristics, the ±10% rule, the veto rule – and turns them into guides, model texts, quizzes and practice tools that students can use on their own.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Progress lives in the student’s own browser and nowhere else.

Use it freely

Everything here may be used in class: project it, share links to individual sections, print or copy the PDFs, put the URL on your Moodle, Google Classroom or MS Teams page. No permission needed, no strings attached. A short mention of the source is appreciated, never required.

Three ideas for your classroom

Task bank as Schularbeit prep
The Matura-style tasks in the Task bank come with source material and operators, ready for timed writing sessions. The random button settles the “which topic” discussion in one click.
Self-check as a peer ritual
Before texts reach your desk, students run them through the Self-check studio and rate themselves on the four official criteria. Peer pairs compare ratings first – the arguments they have are half the learning.
Countdown plan as a frame
The Countdown plan gives the last four weeks before the Klausur a day-by-day structure. Assign it as the homework skeleton and spend lesson time on feedback instead of organisation.

Found a mistake?

Corrections from colleagues are gold. If a fact, a model text or a quiz answer looks off, send a short e-mail naming the section. More about me at bernhardgmeiner.com.

About this site

A free, independent revision site for the written part of the English Matura (AHS & BHS, B2). Made by an English teacher. It is not an official document of the BMB or any education authority.

Your data

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your progress is saved only in this browser on this device, so clearing your browser or switching devices resets it. Nothing leaves your device unless you use the optional AI features yourself. Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) logs your IP address in its server logs.
