Essay~400 words
Overtourism
Your class has discussed how tourism is changing European cities.
The statement you are discussing
From a European travel magazine
"Tourism is eating its own children: the more successful a destination becomes, the less liveable it is for the people who call it home."
Write an essay (around 400 words) discussing this statement. Give your essay a title.
  • describe the economic benefits tourism brings to local communities
  • analyse the damage overtourism causes
  • suggest possible solutions and regulations

Report~250 words
School canteen food quality
The student council surveyed students about the school canteen. The principal has asked you to write up the results.
Survey results — 120 students, years 5–8
  • 58% buy lunch in the canteen at least three times a week
  • Food quality rated "poor" or "very poor" by 44%
  • 71% say healthy options sell out before the lunch break
  • Average waiting time: 14 minutes (the lunch break is 25)
  • 63% would pay up to €1 more for better quality
Write a report (around 250 words) for the principal based on these results.
  • present the main findings of the survey
  • analyse the impact on student health and concentration
  • recommend improvements
