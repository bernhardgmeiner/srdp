About

NotebookLM study companion

An AI study buddy that has read this whole guide. Ask it anything about the writing exam, or just listen to a short explainer on the way to school.

What it is

NotebookLM is a free tool from Google. This notebook has been given the entire content of this guide as its sources, and nothing else. That is the point: it answers from this material and links back to where each answer comes from, so it stays close to what the Matura actually rewards instead of inventing rules. You can chat with it in your own words, or open one of the ready-made explainers.

What is already inside

Audio explainers
A short “How to…” brief for each text type, a longer interactive deep dive on the whole writing exam, a general overview, and a separate episode in German for parents. Press play and listen.
A revision guide
A written study guide that pulls the whole guide into one place, ready to read through in one sitting.
Flashcards
Ready-made flashcards for quick self-testing on vocabulary, text-type conventions and grammar.
A mind map
A visual mind map that shows how the text types, grammar and skills fit together.

How to use it

Open the notebook and sign in with a Google account. Then type a question into the chat, or press play on one of the audio explainers. A few things worth asking:

  • “What is the difference between an essay and an article?”
  • “How do I start a letter to the editor?”
  • “Grade this text like an examiner: …”
  • “Give me a 250-word report task to practise, then a checklist for it.”

Good to know

  • You need to be signed in to a Google account to use it.
  • It is a practice aid, not an official grade. In the real Matura only your teachers assess, using the official scale.
  • Please do not paste real names or personal data into the chat, and follow your school’s own rules on using AI first.
  • The notebook is kept in step with this guide and grows over time, so new explainers and material show up now and then. Worth checking back.

About this site

A free, independent revision site for the written part of the English Matura (AHS & BHS, B2). Made by an English teacher. It is not an official document of the BMB or any education authority.

Your data

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your progress is saved only in this browser on this device, so clearing your browser or switching devices resets it. Nothing leaves your device unless you use the optional AI features yourself. Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) logs your IP address in its server logs.
