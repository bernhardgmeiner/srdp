# Countdown study plans (AHS & BHS)

_This source is part of the "Don't Panic, It's Just the Matura" B2 Writing Guide (matura.bernhardgmeiner.com). It follows the official Austrian SRDP documents for the written English Matura (AHS and BHS, level B2)._

Day-by-day revision schedules for the run-up to the written Matura. Pick the one that matches your school and the time you have left.

## AHS: the four-week plan

Five short sessions a week for four weeks, covering every text type, then two full dress rehearsals.

**Week 1**

**Day 1: Read the rules of the game**

- Read Overview & grading from top to bottom. Slowly. This is the map for everything else. _(25 min)_
- Close the page and write down the four criteria from memory. Check yourself. _(5 min)_

**Day 2: Meet the essay**

- Work through the essay guide: layout, do’s and don’ts, weaker vs. stronger. _(25 min)_
- Take the essay quiz until you pass it. _(10 min)_

**Day 3: Paragraphs are the unit of thought**

- Do the Paragraph writing section: the four layers, then at least two warm-ups. _(30 min)_
- Read the essay model text and find the topic sentence of each paragraph. _(10 min)_

**Day 4: First real essay**

- Pick an essay task from the Task bank and write it. No timer yet, but in one sitting. _(45 min)_

**Day 5: Feedback day**

- Run yesterday’s essay through the Self-check studio and fix what it finds. _(20 min)_
- Look up your two most annoying mistakes in the Grammar kit. _(15 min)_

**Week 2**

**Day 6: The article**

- Work through the article guide and model text. _(25 min)_
- Take the article quiz. _(10 min)_

**Day 7: The report**

- Work through the report guide. Note: headings are mandatory, numbering is not. _(25 min)_
- Take the report quiz. _(10 min)_

**Day 8: Write an article**

- Write a ~250-word article from the Task bank, then self-check it. _(45 min)_

**Day 9: Write a report**

- Write a ~250-word report from the Task bank, then self-check it. _(45 min)_

**Day 10: Upgrade your connectors**

- Learn the B2 linking words table in the Grammar kit – five new ones, used in real sentences. _(20 min)_
- Do one topic in Topic vocabulary as flashcards. _(15 min)_

**Week 3**

**Day 11: The blog**

- Work through the blog guide – register is everything here. _(25 min)_
- Take the blog quiz. _(10 min)_

**Day 12: The e-mail**

- Work through the e-mail guide and all four sub-types. _(30 min)_
- Take the e-mail quiz. _(10 min)_

**Day 13: Write a formal e-mail**

- Write a complaint or application from the Task bank, then self-check it. _(45 min)_

**Day 14: German-to-English traps**

- Do five topics in the Grammar kit: articles, since/for, if-clauses, false friends, word order. _(35 min)_

**Day 15: Phrase day**

- Pick ten phrases from the Phrase bank you will actually use, and copy them into your notes. _(20 min)_
- One more flashcard session – let the boxes do their work. _(15 min)_

**Week 4**

**Day 16: Dress rehearsal I**

- Full exam simulation from the Task bank: one ~400-word and one ~250-word task, 120 minutes, no phone, no dictionary. _(120 min)_

**Day 17: Debrief day**

- Run both rehearsal texts through the Self-check studio, including the AI feedback prompt. _(30 min)_
- Write your personal top-5 mistake list. Keep it next to you this week. _(10 min)_

**Day 18: Practice zone day**

- In the Practice zone: Spot the mistakes, Register gym, and the final quiz. _(40 min)_

**Day 19: Close the gaps**

- Retake your weakest quizzes (the menu shows which sections have no ✓✓ yet). _(25 min)_
- Read the Writing checklist once, slowly. _(15 min)_

**Day 20: Dress rehearsal II**

- One more 120-minute run with two fresh tasks. Then stop revising. Seriously. _(120 min)_

## AHS: the one-week intensive

When the exam is only days away: seven focused days built around the essay and one short text type.
**Day 1: The map and the main event**

- Read Overview & grading – you need to know how points are won and lost. _(25 min)_
- Work through the essay guide and take the quiz. _(35 min)_

**Day 2: Write the essay**

- Write a ~400-word essay from the Task bank in 65 minutes, then self-check it. _(90 min)_

**Day 3: The formal e-mail**

- Work through the e-mail guide with sub-types, take the quiz. _(40 min)_
- Learn the opening/closing pairs by heart – they are free points. _(10 min)_

**Day 4: Your second short type**

- Pick the short type your school favours – article or report – and do guide + quiz. _(40 min)_

**Day 5: Write it under time**

- Write a ~250-word text from the Task bank in 40 minutes, then self-check with AI feedback. _(70 min)_

**Day 6: Sharpen the language**

- Grammar kit top five: articles, since/for, if-clauses, false friends, word order – in the Grammar kit. _(35 min)_
- Take the final quiz. _(15 min)_

**Day 7: Full run, then rest**

- Both tasks, 120 minutes, exam conditions, from the Task bank. _(120 min)_
- Then close this site and sleep. A rested brain beats a crammed one.
