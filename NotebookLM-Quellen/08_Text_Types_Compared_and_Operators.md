# Text types compared, and task operators

_This source is part of the "Don't Panic, It's Just the Matura" B2 Writing Guide (matura.bernhardgmeiner.com). It follows the official Austrian SRDP documents for the written English Matura (AHS and BHS, level B2)._

A side-by-side of the five/six text types, plus what the task command words (discuss, argue, evaluate...) actually ask for.

## Task operators (command words)

Read the operator in your task carefully. It changes what a good answer looks like.

**discuss** — Look at a topic from different angles. Present arguments for and against, weigh them up.

_Example move:_ There are strong arguments both for and against… On the one hand…

**evaluate** — Examine something and judge its quality or value. Is it good/bad, effective or not – and why?

_Example move:_ While this approach has certain merits, it fails to address…

**argue (for/against)** — Take a clear position and support it. Stronger than "discuss" – you are not neutral.

_Example move:_ I firmly believe that… The most convincing reason for this is…

**analyse** — Break something down into its parts or causes. How and why does it work (or not)?

_Example move:_ The main factors contributing to this are… A closer look reveals that…

**compare** — Point out similarities and differences between two or more things.

_Example move:_ While X tends to…, Y is more likely to… Both share…

**describe** — Give a clear picture of something – facts, features, what happened.

_Example move:_ The first thing visitors notice is… The programme consists of…

**suggest / recommend** — Propose ideas or solutions. What should be done?

_Example move:_ I would recommend that… One possible solution would be to…

**explain** — Make something clear. Say how or why something happens.

_Example move:_ The reason for this is that… This can be explained by the fact that…

## General writing checklist (any text type)

- [ ] Read the task twice – underlined text type, audience, word count, and all bullet points.
- [ ] Bullet point 1 is addressed with a full paragraph of development.
- [ ] Bullet point 2 is addressed with a full paragraph of development.
- [ ] Bullet point 3 is addressed with a full paragraph of development.
- [ ] Word count is within ±10% of the number the task states (for a 250-word task, 225–275).
- [ ] Every body paragraph starts with a clear topic sentence.
- [ ] Used at least 5 different B2 linking words (Furthermore, However, Consequently…).
- [ ] Did NOT copy words or phrases directly from the task prompt.
- [ ] Used at least 2 complex structures (passive voice, conditional, relative clause).
- [ ] Proofread for personal weaknesses (articles, third-person -s, tenses).
