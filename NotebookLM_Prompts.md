# Prompt-Sammlung für dein Matura-NotebookLM

Fertige Prompts zum Kopieren, sortiert nach Ausgabeformat. Diese Datei ist für dich gedacht, nicht zum Hochladen als Quelle.

**Wo kommt was hin?**

- Audio: beim Audio Overview auf „Customize" klicken und den Prompt ins Fokus-Feld einfügen.
- Video: beim Video Overview auf „Customize" klicken, Prompt einfügen, ggf. Quellen auswählen.
- Chat: einfach ins Chat-Feld unten.
- Reports/Study Guide: im „Studio"- bzw. Reports-Bereich als eigenen Report-Prompt.
- Sprache: Für deutsche Audios/Videos die Output-Sprache in den NotebookLM-Einstellungen auf Deutsch stellen. Die meisten Prompts hier sind auf Englisch, damit die Ausgabe englisch ist – die deutschen Varianten sind extra markiert.

Wo `[ESSAY]`, `[REPORT]` usw. steht, tausch die Textsorte einfach aus (essay, article, report, blog, email, leaflet).

---

## 1. Explainer-Audio (Audio Overview / Deep Dive)

**Kompakter Gesamtüberblick**
```
Create a Deep Dive audio overview for Austrian secondary-school students preparing for the written English Matura at B2. Assume they know nothing yet. Cover how the exam works for AHS and BHS, the four assessment criteria, the plus-or-minus 10% word rule and the veto rule, and give a one-line takeaway for each text type. Keep it encouraging and concrete, use examples from the model texts in the sources, and stay under 12 minutes. Speak in English at a level a B2 learner can follow.
```

**Eine Folge pro Textsorte** (Textsorte im Prompt austauschen)
```
Make an audio deep dive on ONE text type only: the [ESSAY]. Explain its purpose and reader, its structure, its register, the key do's and don'ts, and walk through the annotated model text for this type, pointing out what each part achieves. End with the three mistakes that cost the most marks. English, about 8 minutes, aimed at a B2 student.
```

**Grammatik-Fallen als Episode**
```
Audio overview focused only on the Grammar Kit source. Turn the 14 German-into-English traps into a lively, memorable episode: for each, the wrong version, the right version and a quick rule of thumb. Give the most weight to the highest-frequency ones: articles, since versus for, if-clauses, false friends and word order. English, upbeat, about 10 minutes.
```

**Wie Prüfer:innen wirklich lesen**
```
Audio deep dive based on the "Grade like an examiner" and grading sources. Explain how a rater actually reads a text: expectation horizon first, one criterion at a time, the Task Achievement veto, a band is a judgement not an average, and the length penalty. Use one of the six worked examples to show why the same text can score 9 for Range and 6 for Task Achievement. English, about 9 minutes.
```

**Deutsch erklärt, englische Beispiele** (Output-Sprache auf Deutsch stellen)
```
Erstelle das Audio Overview auf Deutsch. Erkläre einer AHS- oder BHS-Schülerin, wie die schriftliche Englisch-Matura bewertet wird und wie man jede Textsorte aufbaut. Alle Beispielsätze und festen Wendungen bleiben auf Englisch, erklärt und kommentiert wird auf Deutsch. Rund 10 Minuten, ruhig und ermutigend.
```

**Nacht vor der Prüfung**
```
Make a short, calming "night before the exam" audio: no new content, just the essentials that win cheap marks. Read the task twice, plan your finishing times, hit the word count, match greeting to sign-off, use five different linkers, and proofread your personal top errors. Confident and reassuring, English, under 6 minutes.
```

**Die häufigsten Fehler, freundlich seziert**
```
Audio overview based on the "Realistic texts and common mistakes" source. Playfully diagnose the six flawed student texts: which genre each one accidentally became, and the single fix that would rescue it. Warm, never mean. English, about 8 minutes.
```

**Register: formell vs. informell**
```
Audio deep dive on register: when contractions, rhetorical questions and personal stories help (blog, article) and when they sink you (essay, report, formal e-mail). Use the register-gym transformations in the sources as examples. English, about 7 minutes.
```

**Dialog/Debatte zwischen den Hosts**
```
Audio overview as a friendly debate between the two hosts: one argues the essay is the hardest task, the other argues the formal e-mail is. Have them use evidence from the sources and land on one piece of practical advice for each text type. English, about 8 minutes.
```

**Nur die Modelltexte**
```
Audio deep dive that reads and unpacks the annotated model texts across all text types. For each, point out the hook, the structure, the strongest sentence and one thing a student could copy. English, about 11 minutes.
```

## 3. Textsorten-Beschreiber (Chat)

**Master-Vorlage** (beide Textsorten austauschen)
```
You are a B2 Matura writing coach. Describe the text type [ESSAY] using only the sources. Give me: (1) purpose and reader in one line, (2) target length and register, (3) the layout part by part, (4) the five do's and five don'ts that matter most, (5) three signature phrases, (6) the one mistake that most often ruins it, and (7) two lines on how it differs from the [ARTICLE]. Keep it to one page, in English.
```

**Essay auf einen Blick**
```
Using only the sources, give me a one-page describer of the ESSAY: purpose, length and register, paragraph-by-paragraph structure, five do's, five don'ts, three phrases I can reuse, and the single mistake that most often costs marks. End with one line: "An essay makes the reader think; an article makes the reader feel." English.
```

**Die vier E-Mail-Sub-Types**
```
Using the sources, give me a one-page describer of the four formal e-mail sub-types: complaint, application, letter to the editor, and enquiry. For each: its purpose, the one obligatory opening move, the correct greeting and sign-off, and one example phrase. English.
```

**Blog Post vs. Blog Comment**
```
Explain the difference between a blog POST and a blog COMMENT for the Matura: title or no title, how each one opens, and how each one closes. Give me a two-sentence model opening for each. From the sources, in English.
```

**Leaflet gegen Report abgrenzen**
```
From the sources, explain how a LEAFLET differs from a REPORT on the same event: purpose, register, addressing the reader, headings, and call to action. Give one example opening line for each so the contrast is obvious. English.
```

**Alle Textsorten in einer Tabelle**
```
Build me a single comparison table of all text types with rows for: contractions, personal examples, rhetorical questions, section headings, recommendations, addressing the reader, and title. Fill it strictly from the sources. English.
```

## 5. Üben, testen, korrigieren lassen (Chat)

**Aufgaben-Generator** (Textsorte austauschen)
```
Act as my Matura examiner. Give me one realistic [ESSAY] task in the exam format: situation, prompt, three bullet points and a word count, modelled on the Task Bank source. Then wait for my text before saying anything else.
```

**Korrigieren wie eine Prüferin**
```
Grade the text I paste next like an Austrian B2 rater. Follow the method in the AI Feedback Prompt source: expectation horizon first, then each of the four criteria with a band from 0 to 10 and quoted evidence, then the five most important errors as a table (quote, correction, one-line reason), then a holistic verdict and one thing I did well. Do not rewrite my text.
```

**Quiz mich zu Konventionen**
```
Quiz me on text-type conventions. Ask me 10 multiple-choice questions one at a time, drawn from the quizzes in the sources. Wait for my answer each time, tell me right or wrong and why, and give me a score at the end.
```

**Register-Drills**
```
Give me 10 informal sentences and ask me to rewrite each one in formal exam English. After each of my attempts, show the model formal version and one short tip. One sentence at a time.
```

**Spot-the-mistake-Generator** (Textsorte austauschen)
```
Write a short B2 student text in the [REPORT] genre that deliberately breaks three conventions from the sources. Ask me to find them. Once I answer, reveal all three with the fixes.
```

**Satz-Upgrade B1 zu B2**
```
I will paste plain B1 sentences. Rewrite each into a B2 version that shows range (passive, conditional, relative clause or inversion) and name which structure you used. Wait for my sentences.
```

**Absatz-Trainer**
```
Give me a topic and one supporting idea. Ask me to write a single body paragraph using the four-layer method from the Paragraph Writing source. Then evaluate my paragraph layer by layer and show a stronger model version.
```

**Planungs-Coach**
```
Give me one essay task, then coach me through planning it in five minutes: thesis, three topic sentences, and one piece of evidence for each. Do not write the essay for me; push me with questions until my plan is solid.
```

**Titel-Werkstatt**
```
Give me five article topics. For each, ask me to write a catchy title, then compare mine with two strong options (a rhetorical question and a bold statement) and say why they work.
```

## 8. Feinsteuerung

Ein paar Stellschrauben, die fast jeden Prompt besser machen:

- Länge: „about 8 minutes", „under 200 words", „one page".
- Ton: „encouraging", „calm", „upbeat", „formal and neutral".
- Fokus auf eine Quelle: im Chat links die gewünschte Quelle auswählen, dann antwortet NotebookLM nur daraus. Praktisch für „nur Essay" oder „nur Grammar Kit".
- Zielgruppe immer mitgeben: „for an Austrian B2 Matura student (AHS/BHS)" hält das Niveau richtig.
- Beispiele erzwingen: „use concrete examples from the model texts" verhindert schwammige Antworten.
