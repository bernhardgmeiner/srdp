# Task bank: 32 Matura-style writing prompts

_This source is part of the "Don't Panic, It's Just the Matura" B2 Writing Guide (matura.bernhardgmeiner.com). It follows the official Austrian SRDP documents for the written English Matura (AHS and BHS, level B2)._

Practice prompts in the real exam format, most with source material (statements, survey data, adverts, blog posts to react to). Write to time, then self-check.

## Essay tasks

### Essay — Overtourism (~400 words)

**Situation:** Your class has discussed how tourism is changing European cities.

**The statement you are discussing (From a European travel magazine):**

> "Tourism is eating its own children: the more successful a destination becomes, the less liveable it is for the people who call it home."

**Task:** Write an essay (around 400 words) discussing this statement. Give your essay a title.

**In your text you should:**

- describe the economic benefits tourism brings to local communities
- analyse the damage overtourism causes
- suggest possible solutions and regulations

### Essay — Social media and mental health (~400 words)

**Situation:** A youth organisation has invited students to submit essays for its annual publication.

**The statement you are discussing (From a youth magazine):**

> "Social media has given teenagers a thousand new friends and taken away their peace of mind."

**Task:** Write an essay (around 400 words) discussing this statement. Give your essay a title.

**In your text you should:**

- describe positive aspects of social media use
- analyse negative effects on self-image and wellbeing
- evaluate the responsibilities of platforms and parents

### Essay — School uniforms (~400 words)

**Situation:** Your school is considering introducing a uniform and has asked students for written opinions.

**The statement you are discussing (From a student newspaper):**

> "School uniforms do not create equality; they only hide inequality until the school bell rings."

**Task:** Write an essay (around 400 words) arguing for or against mandatory school uniforms. Give your essay a title.

**In your text you should:**

- present arguments in favour of uniforms (equality, focus)
- present arguments against them (expression, cost)
- state and justify your own position

### Essay — Voting at 16 (~400 words)

**Situation:** An international student magazine is collecting essays on democracy and young people.

**The statement you are discussing (Heard at a student debate):**

> "If you are old enough to pay taxes on your apprentice wage, you are old enough to vote on what happens with them."

**Task:** Write an essay (around 400 words) discussing whether 16-year-olds should be allowed to vote in national elections. Give your essay a title.

**In your text you should:**

- explain why young people's voices matter in politics
- discuss concerns about maturity and experience
- evaluate Austria's experience with voting at 16

### Essay — Artificial intelligence in school (~400 words)

**Situation:** Your school's debating club is publishing essays about technology in education.

**The statement you are discussing (From an education blog):**

> "A generation that lets AI do its homework will be the first that knows less than its parents."

**Task:** Write an essay (around 400 words) discussing this statement. Give your essay a title.

**In your text you should:**

- describe how students currently use AI tools
- analyse the risks for learning and fairness
- suggest rules schools should introduce

### Essay — Part-time jobs for teenagers (~400 words)

**Situation:** A parents' association has asked for student perspectives on working while at school.

**The statement you are discussing (Overheard at a parents' evening):**

> "A Saturday job teaches a teenager more about real life than a whole year of school."

**Task:** Write an essay (around 400 words) discussing whether teenagers should have part-time jobs. Give your essay a title.

**In your text you should:**

- describe the benefits of work experience
- analyse possible negative effects on schoolwork
- state your own conclusion with reasons

## Report tasks

### Report — School canteen food quality (~250 words)

**Situation:** The student council surveyed students about the school canteen. The principal has asked you to write up the results.

**Survey results – 120 students, years 5–8:**

> 58% buy lunch in the canteen at least three times a week
> Food quality rated "poor" or "very poor" by 44%
> 71% say healthy options sell out before the lunch break
> Average waiting time: 14 minutes (the lunch break is 25)
> 63% would pay up to €1 more for better quality

**Task:** Write a report (around 250 words) for the principal based on these results.

**In your text you should:**

- present the main findings of the survey
- analyse the impact on student health and concentration
- recommend improvements

### Report — Technology for learning (~400 words)

**Situation:** Your school wants to review how students use technology and has collected questionnaire data.

**Questionnaire results – 210 students, years 4–8:**

> 92% use a laptop or tablet for schoolwork every day
> 47% say lessons rarely make use of digital materials
> Most common uses: research (78%), vocabulary apps (54%), AI tools (49%)
> 38% admit devices often distract them in class
> Only 25% have ever been trained in checking online sources

**Task:** Write a report (around 400 words) for the school management team based on these results.

**In your text you should:**

- present current usage patterns
- analyse benefits and problems the data shows
- recommend guidelines for future use

### Report — Sports facilities (~250 words)

**Situation:** Your town council asked young residents about local sports facilities. You volunteered to summarise the answers.

**Survey results – 85 residents aged 14–19:**

> 66% use local sports facilities at least once a week
> The skate park was rated "in poor condition" by 7 out of 10 users
> Only 2 of the 6 outdoor pitches have working floodlights
> 81% would use the sports hall more if there were evening slots

**Task:** Write a report (around 250 words) for the town council based on these results.

**In your text you should:**

- present what young people think of the existing facilities
- identify the main problems the data shows
- suggest two realistic improvements

### Report — School trip evaluation (~250 words)

**Situation:** After this year's project week, students completed feedback forms. Your teacher asked you to write up the results.

**Feedback results – 52 students, project week:**

> Overall rating: 3.1 out of 5
> Best rated: outdoor activities (4.4 / 5)
> Worst rated: accommodation (1.9 / 5) and food (2.2 / 5)
> 60% found the daily programme "too full"
> 88% would join another project week

**Task:** Write a report (around 250 words) for your form teacher based on these results.

**In your text you should:**

- summarise how students rated the project week
- present the most common criticisms
- recommend changes for next year

### Report — Guest / customer feedback (~250 words)

**Situation:** You work part-time at a hotel. Management surveyed guests and asked you to summarise the results in English for the international owners.

**Guest survey – 140 guests, past season:**

> Overall satisfaction: 3.4 out of 5
> Best rated: friendliness of staff (4.5) and breakfast (4.1)
> Worst rated: Wi-Fi (2.1) and room cleanliness (2.6)
> 61% would recommend the hotel to others
> 48% found check-in too slow at peak times

**Task:** Write a report (around 250 words) for the hotel owners based on these results.

**In your text you should:**

- summarise the main findings under clear section headings
- analyse the strengths and the problem areas
- recommend concrete improvements

## Blog tasks

### Blog — Living without a car (~400 words)

### Blog — Comment: school start times (~250 words)

**Situation:** You follow a popular education blog and want to react to its latest post.

**The blog post you are commenting on (jamie_teaches · "School should start at 10 a.m. – change my mind" · posted 2 days ago):**

> "Teenagers' body clocks simply aren't made for 8 a.m. lessons. Research keeps telling us that adolescents need more sleep and perform better later in the day, yet schools keep dragging students out of bed at dawn. Start at 10, finish at 5, and everyone wins: better grades, better moods, fewer zombies in first period. Am I wrong?"

**Task:** Write a comment (around 250 words) responding to Jamie's post. Do not give your comment a title.

**In your text you should:**

- react to the original post
- share your own experience with early school starts
- state what you think schools should do

### Blog — Comment: fast food ban near schools (~250 words)

**Situation:** A food blogger you follow has published a post that is getting a lot of reactions.

**The blog post you are commenting on (lena.eats.well · "Keep burgers away from schools!" · posted yesterday):**

> "I'm serious: fast food restaurants should be banned within 500 metres of every school. Walk past any school gate at lunchtime and you'll see queues of teenagers buying burgers, fries and energy drinks. We teach nutrition in biology and then let the burger chains undo it all at lunch. Distance rules work for casinos, so why not for junk food?"

**Task:** Write a comment (around 250 words) responding to Lena's post. Do not give your comment a title.

**In your text you should:**

- respond directly to the blogger's argument
- describe what you observe at your own school
- suggest a better (or stricter) solution

## Email tasks

### Email — Complaint: language course (~250 words)

**Situation:** You attended a two-week language course in Brighton that did not match what the website promised.

**What the website promised (globalenglish.example – Summer Intensive Brighton):**

> "Small groups of maximum 10 students · all teachers fully qualified with at least five years' experience · modern single rooms with private bathroom, five minutes from the school · full social programme included"

**Task:** Write an e-mail (around 250 words) to the course provider.

**In your text you should:**

- compare what was promised with what you actually experienced
- describe the problems with the accommodation
- state what you want the company to do

### Email — Letter to the editor: electric cars (~250 words)

**Situation:** You have read an article in an English-language newspaper and strongly disagree with it.

**From the article you are responding to ("The Electric Car Illusion" – The Daily Courier, 3 February 2026):**

> "Electric cars are a pointless trend that will change nothing. They are toys for wealthy city dwellers: too expensive for ordinary families, useless without charging stations, and powered by electricity that still comes from fossil fuels. Politicians who subsidise them are wasting taxpayers' money."

**Task:** Write a letter to the editor (around 250 words) responding to the article.

**In your text you should:**

- respond to the article's main claims
- present your view on the environmental benefits
- discuss the practical challenges of switching

### Email — Application: summer internship (~250 words)

**Situation:** You have found an advertisement on the website of an international environmental NGO.

Apply by e-mail to Ms Hana Said, including your relevant experience and why you want to join us."

### Email — Letter to the editor: city park (~250 words)

**Situation:** Your local English-language newspaper has reported on plans that affect young people in your district.

**From the article you are responding to ("Parking Garage to Replace Westpark" – Vienna Daily News, 14 March 2026):**

> "The city council confirmed on Thursday that Westpark will make way for a four-storey parking garage with 600 spaces. A spokesperson called the park 'underused green space' and pointed to the chronic lack of parking in the district. Construction could begin as early as next spring."

**Task:** Write a letter to the editor (around 250 words) about these plans.

**In your text you should:**

- refer to the newspaper report
- explain what the park means to young people in your area
- appeal to readers and the city council

### Email — Business enquiry to a supplier (~250 words)

**Situation:** During your practical training in a company's purchasing department, your supervisor asks you to contact an English-speaking supplier.

**Your supervisor's note (Internal note):**

> "Please ask GreenPack Ltd for current prices for recycled packaging, minimum order quantities and delivery times to Austria. We need the goods by mid-October."

**Task:** Write a formal e-mail of enquiry (around 250 words) to the supplier on behalf of your company.

**In your text you should:**

- introduce yourself and your company briefly
- ask about prices, minimum order quantities and delivery times
- state your deadline and ask for a prompt reply

### Email — Application answering a job ad (~250 words)

**Situation:** You have found an advertisement for a summer job that fits your BHS training.

**The advertisement (alpinehotels.example – Summer Reception Assistant):**

> "We are looking for a reception assistant for July and August. Tasks: welcoming guests, answering e-mails and phone calls in German and English, simple bookings. We expect good English, a friendly manner and reliability. Apply by e-mail to Mr Berger with your relevant experience."

**Task:** Write an e-mail of application (around 250 words) to Mr Berger. Keep the register formal throughout.

**In your text you should:**

- state which position you are applying for and where you saw it
- describe your relevant skills, training and experience
- explain why you are suitable and offer to attend an interview

## Leaflet tasks

**Situation:** Your school is holding an open day for pupils from local secondary schools and their parents. Your English teacher has asked you to write the text for the leaflet.

**Task:** Write a leaflet (around 250 words). Give it a title and use meaningful subheadings.

**In your text you should:**

- explain what visitors can see and do on the day
- describe what makes your school worth choosing
- give the practical details and invite readers to come

- explain how the new scheme works
- describe why it is worth taking part
