Leaving school early

Give a five-minute talk on the topic of school and education in which you

  1. interpret the information in the chart,
  2. outline some of the reasons why young people leave education early,

Printable task cards

The cards are packed onto A4 pages, usually two to a page. Charts print with their source. Pictures are AI-generated and marked as such.

Ideas for a lesson

One task, the whole class
Show a Long Turn, run the preparation clock, and let everybody write keywords. Then one or two volunteers give the talk, and the class says whether the speaker covered all three bullets, gave an example and gave a reason.
Pairs with their phones
Pairs open the trainer and do a Paired Activity with one phone between them. You walk round and listen. Afterwards each pair names one thing it wants to do better.
Mock exam as homework
Two students do the full mock exam together, at home or in a free period at school, and bring one sentence each: what the partner did well.

About the tasks

These are practice tasks, written for this site. None of them is an exam task, and none comes from a school’s task pool. About this site

A free, independent site for the text types of the written English Matura (AHS and BHS, B2), from the 5th class to the Matura, with a trainer for the oral exam. Made by an English teacher. It is not an official document of the BMB or of any education authority.

For teachers: Writing Room, a projector tool for writing model texts together in class.
