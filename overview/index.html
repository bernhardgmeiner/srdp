Overview & grading

The Writing section

You have 120 minutes for two tasks: one longer (~400 words, often an essay, but it can be another text type) and one shorter (~250 words). Writing is one of four equally weighted sections of the written exam, alongside Reading, Listening and Language in Use.

A time split that works: ~65 min for the 400-word task (incl. 5–10 min planning) · ~40 min for the 250-word task · ~15 min to proofread both. Write your finishing times on the task sheet before you start.
Text typeWord countRegisterTitle?Key feature
Essay~400 words (360–440)FormalRequiredArgue your position with evidence
Article~250 or ~400 wordsDepends on the audienceRequired – catchy!Write for real readers
Report~250 or ~400 wordsFormal and neutralSubject line + section headingsPresent findings and recommend action
Blog~250 or ~400 wordsInformal to semi-formalPost only (not a comment)Personal voice, invite interaction
E-mail~250 words (225–275)Usually formalRequiredClear purpose, proper format
Word count tolerance: ±10%. That is 360–440 words for 400-word tasks and 225–275 for 250-word tasks. If your text is more than 10% too long or too short, Task Achievement drops by one band.
Almost every task lists three content points (the three bullet points in the task). Cover each one, usually in its own body paragraph.
Register means how formal or casual your language is. Compare a letter to a company with a message to a friend.
Practise with real exams too. The tasks on this site are written in the Matura style. The official past papers are in the BMB download area at matura.gv.at/downloads.

How you are graded – the four criteria

Each text is rated with the official scale: four criteria, each scored 0–10 and weighted equally. A weaker criterion can be balanced by a stronger one.

01
Task Achievement
Erfüllung der Aufgabenstellung
All content points covered with supporting detail; text type conventions followed; appropriate title/greeting/closing; word count within ±10%.
02
Coherence and Cohesion
Kohärenz und Kohäsion
Clear overall structure; effective paragraphing; logical development; variety of linking words; clear relationships between sentences.
03
Lexical and Structural Range
Spektrum sprachlicher Mittel
Variety of vocabulary and structures; complex sentence forms; not repeating the same phrases; appropriate register; not lifting from the prompt.
04
Lexical and Structural Accuracy
Sprachrichtigkeit
Grammar control; correct word choice; spelling; punctuation; accurate use of linking words.
VETO rule: if a text does not address the set task at all (an off-topic or clearly pre-prepared text that ignores the actual prompt), Task Achievement is rated 0 and the other three criteria are not assessed. Writing in the wrong text type alone does not trigger this. Read every task twice.
Don’t play it safe with grammar. A text built only on simple sentences cannot reach the upper Range bands, even if every sentence is correct. Work in a passive, a conditional or a relative clause where it fits naturally.

What the bands mean (simplified)

Everything the task asks for, fully developed with well-chosen details. Conventions confidently used, wide range of vocabulary and complex structures, only occasional slips.

All content points developed with relevant details. Clear structure, good range and variety, errors are rare and never cause misunderstanding.

This is the B2 minimum (6/10): all content points developed but with limited supporting detail, a limited but appropriate set of linking words, enough range including some complex forms, and errors that do not get in the way of communication.

Content points addressed but not developed, mostly simple linear sentences, repetition and lifting from the prompt, or errors pile up beyond simple structures.

Only parts of the task recognisable, very limited language, errors make parts of the text hard to understand.

No assessable performance – or the text fails to address the set task (see the veto rule above).

Simplified from the official B2 assessment scale (BMB, 2023 revision). The scale describes bands 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10. The odd-numbered bands (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) are for texts between two descriptions. Your teachers grade with the full official scale.

From bands to your grade

  1. Your two texts get 8 band scores (2 texts × 4 criteria, each 0–10). That is 80 points at most, and both texts count the same.
  2. They are converted into the 25 points of the Writing section: your total ÷ 80 × 25. Reading, listening and language in use count 25 points each.
  3. To pass, you need at least 60 of 100 points overall and at least 25 points in each area: reading + listening, and language in use + writing.
Points (of 100)Grade (Note)
90 or moreSehr gut
80 or moreGut
70 or moreBefriedigend
60 or moreGenügend
under 60, or under 25 in one areaNicht genügend
Example: band 6 on all eight criteria is 48 of 80, which gives 15 of 25 points (60%). Band 6 is the B2 minimum. A weaker text can still be balanced by the other parts of the exam.

Source: the official correction and assessment guide for the written exam in modern foreign languages (BMB/IQS, September 2024). Your teachers apply it. If anything is unclear, ask them.

The 8 most expensive mistakes

Each one is a habit you can change.

MistakeWhat it costsThe fix
Writing only 2 of 3 content pointsTask Achievement drops sharply→ Tick off each content point as you write it
Ignoring the text type’s rules (e.g. a report without headings)Task Achievement drops – conventions are part of the criterion→ Check the Layout box in the guide before you start
Copying words from the prompt or the input materialRange drops: copied language does not count as your own→ Rephrase, then interpret: what does the figure mean for your point?
Word count more than 10% too long or too shortTask Achievement drops one band→ Count your words. Practise estimating.
Formal style in a blog (or vice versa)Wrong register costs marks (it is not counted as an Accuracy error)→ Decide: formal or informal? Then keep to it.
Only simple linking wordsLow Coherence score→ Use the B2 linking words table
No topic sentencesParagraphs feel random, weak Coherence→ First sentence of each paragraph = the point
Forgetting to proofreadErrors you would catch in 3 minutes→ Keep about 15 minutes at the end
